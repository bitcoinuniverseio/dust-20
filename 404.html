    404.1 What probably happened

    Common causes and where to go instead
    CauseWhere to go

    You followed a link to a page that was renamed in document version 1.1.0.

    The specification now holds the numbered rules, and the reference holds the field ledger and formulas.

    You were looking for a rule identifier such as DUST-6.7.

    Every rule is an anchor on the specification page, for example specification.html#DUST-6.7.

    You were looking for a conformance case.

    Every vector is an anchor on the test vectors page, and the full set is at conformance.json.

    You wanted to know whether DUST-20 can be traded in Bitcoin Universe.

    It cannot. Marketplace availability is read-only, and the Universe support page publishes the recorded reason.

    You typed the address by hand.

    Press / to search the whole site, or use the index in the sidebar.
