    Safety

    Staying safe with DUST-20

    Bitcoin transactions cannot be undone, and DUST-20 adds a failure mode ordinary payments do not have: a transaction can confirm perfectly while destroying your balance. Know what you are approving.

    Never share secrets

    !

    No DUST-20 application needs your recovery phrase or private key

    An app can prepare an unsigned transaction without them; your wallet signs it on your device. If any site asks for a seed phrase or private key, leave immediately. This documentation site never asks for either and never signs anything.

    App to wallet

    The app should show the receiver, amounts, colored change, network and fee before your wallet appears. The wallet screen is a second check, not the first one.

    App to you

    You should be able to explain every output before approving. If an output has no explanation, that is the problem, not your understanding.

    Balance service

    An indexer gives an interpretation of Bitcoin. If it is missing, stale, or disagrees with another source, stop before a risky send.

    Before you approve

    A PSBT is simply the unsigned transaction your wallet shows before approval. Compare it against what the app told you.

    CheckWhy it mattersWhat a safe app shows
    Network and address A valid-looking address can still be wrong for the network you are on. The network, the receiver address in full, and a hard block on any mismatch.
    What is being spent Colored backing and fee money must not be confused. Each colored input with its ticker, units and satoshis, and the cardinal inputs separately.
    What the receiver gets You need to see where the units are going. Address, satoshis and units for every colored output.
    Your colored change Missing change destroys the units you meant to keep. An explicit colored change output whenever you selected more than you are sending.
    Fee and fee funding The miner fee must not consume colored backing. The fee, which cardinal input pays it, and ordinary change — all listed separately.
    Freshness The selected output may have changed since the app loaded. The balance source and time, re-checked immediately before the unsigned transaction is built.

    For builders

    Most readers can stop at the checklist above. These are the failure modes an application must actively defend against.

    PSBT substitution

    Decode and compare every output role before prompting the wallet. Reject any response that cannot be explained by the review model you showed the user.

    Colored fee leak

    Assert conservation of colored backing and source fee funding from cardinal UTXOs. Treat an unexplained satoshi difference as a failure, never a rounding artifact.

    Stale listing

    Revalidate outpoint and listing state immediately before construction. A local marketplace record is not chain truth.

    Indexer disagreement

    Do not choose the friendlier result. Surface the conflict, block the mutation, and retain redacted evidence for investigation.

    Key exposure

    Never log or request seed material. If private key material could reach a service or telemetry system, disable the affected flow.

    Retry confusion

    Retain the transaction id and known status. Disable blind resubmission until a human has inspected the transaction.

    If something goes wrong

    • Pause the narrowest affected mutation — buy, transfer, split, merge — rather than pretending the system is healthy.
    • Capture redacted context: transaction ids, outpoints, route, build version, source timestamps and error classification. Never collect seeds or private keys.
    • Tell users what is paused and what is known, including whether a transaction may still be pending and how to inspect it.
    • Re-enable only with a regression test, a documented root cause and a fixture that reproduces the failure.
    i

    Documentation does not make a flow safe

    Test the constructions that destroy units, not only the ones that work. The failure modes table and conformance.json are a good starting fixture set.

    DUST

    DUST-20

    DUST-20 binds every token unit to an exact number of satoshis. Deploy and mint are inscriptions; every later movement is an ordinary Bitcoin spend of the satoshis that carry the units.
