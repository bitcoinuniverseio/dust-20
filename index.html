Why DUST-20

Value you can see in the transaction.

DUST-20 turns satoshi relationships into a visible part of the asset experience. The token and the Bitcoin output carrying it are designed to be understood together.

01 / TANGIBLE

Satoshis are part of the design

Each unit follows an explicit satoshi relationship, making its Bitcoin connection easy to recognize.

02 / NATIVE

Ownership lives in outputs

The UTXO carrying an allocation matters, keeping ownership grounded in Bitcoin's native model.

03 / DURABLE

A story written on Bitcoin

Creation and movement join a public history that communities can follow over time.

✓

Your keys. Your allocation. Your decision.

Before signing, check the ticker, amount, satoshi value, recipient, change, and miner fee shown by your wallet. Use a compatible wallet and keep token-carrying outputs separate from fee funding unless the transaction clearly shows how every unit is allocated.
