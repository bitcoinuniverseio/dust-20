# DUST-20

Tokens with Bitcoin at their core.

DUST-20 connects every token unit to an explicit amount of satoshis. The result is a tangible UTXO-based asset model where supply, value, and movement meet directly on Bitcoin.

## Explore

- [Enter DUST-20](https://bitcoinuniverse.github.io/dust-20/)
- [Create with Bitcoin Universe](https://inscribe.bitcoinuniverse.io)
- [Discover the ecosystem](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverse)

## Why it feels different

- **Satoshis are part of the design.** Each unit follows a declared satoshi relationship.
- **Ownership lives in Bitcoin outputs.** The UTXO carrying the allocation matters.
- **The story stays visible.** Creation and movement become part of Bitcoin's public history.

## Stay in control

Before signing, check the ticker, amount, satoshi value, recipient, change, and miner fee shown by your wallet. Use a compatible wallet and keep token-carrying outputs separate from fee funding unless the transaction clearly shows how every unit is allocated.
